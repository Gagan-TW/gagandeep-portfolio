## How I work

- **I write for the reader first.** Before I start, I work out who the reader is, what they already know, and what they need to do.
- **I test what I document.** In my tutorials, I run every step myself before I publish it.
- **I treat AI as a first draft.** A human, me, checks every AI-generated page against the source.
