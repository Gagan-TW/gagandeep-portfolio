# Help Readers With the Right Voice and Tone

Last year, I tried to sign up for an image capture app. I was excited about its features, but one unclear error message was enough to make me lose interest.

I typed my email again and again, and the app kept showing the same message: "Invalid input." I checked for typos. Still the same message. Finally, I tried clicking **Sign in** instead, and it worked. My email was already registered.

But by then, the app had cost me my time, my patience, and my interest.
